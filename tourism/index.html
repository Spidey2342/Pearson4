ABOUT US

The Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture is dedicated to promoting Ghana as a premier destination rich in cultural heritage, vibrant arts and diverse tourist attractions. Initiatives such as the ‘Year of Return’ in 2019 and the “Beyond the Return” campaign have successfully attracted global attention, encouraging the African diaspora abd international tourists to explore Ghana’s unique offerings. Building on the momentum, MoTAC aims to launch a new campaign targeting both domestic and international audiences to showcase lesser-known attractions and cultural experience across the country.

Agbeli Kaklo:

In the Ewe Language spoken by the Ewes of Ghana,
'agbeli' means 'cassava' and hence the word 'agbeli kaklo' means 'cassava fritters'.

Kelewele

‘Kelewele’ is a popular Gluten free, Ghanaian street food snack made from ripened Plantain and ground spices.

Abolo and pepper

Yakayeke

"Yakeyake," a steamed grated cassava dish, is a staple food in the Volta Region of Ghana, particularly among the Ewe people, and is known for its cultural significance and nutritional benefits.
